Metric Interpretation Guide

ROUGE-1: Overlap of unigrams (single words). Measures lexical coverage and informativeness.
ROUGE-2: Overlap of bigrams (two-word sequences). Measures grammatical coherence and phrasing fidelity.
ROUGE-L: Longest Common Subsequence (LCS). Evaluates sentence-level structure without requiring contiguous matches.
